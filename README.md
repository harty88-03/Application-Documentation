# Study Sync

### Study Sync is a study tracker, focus, and study application that incorporates every aspect
### of studying into one application for efficiency. Its users are students who desire proper
### focus and structure. It allows students to keep track of their progress, study effectively,
### and plan their study sessions for the week or month. It also helps reduce the stress of
### planning and supports students who struggle to maintain focus by offering multiple study
### options based on their preferences. By bringing these features together in one application,
### Study Sync aims to reduce the time students spend planning their studies by 20%
