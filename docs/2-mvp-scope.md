# MVP Scope & Feature Backlog 

## The Ultimate Product Vision
Study Sync is an all-in-one study application designed to help students manage their studies.
It allows users to track progress, organize study schedules, and stay focused through personalized study options.

## The Launch Goal (v1.0 MVP)

To launch a functional version by **may 3,2027** focusing strictly on core value.

## Feature Prioritization Matrix

*   **Must Have (MVP Lineup):**

    *   `Study progress tracking`: [Tracks students' study progress and completed tasks. This an essential feature for every study app available as it directly works with the focus session feature to allow users to be         aware how long they worked or took a break ]

    *   `Focus sessions`: [Helps students stay focused and study more effectively. Since focus sessions are a common feature in many study apps, they are essential for the app to function as an effective study app ]

    *   `Personalized study options`: [Offers different study methods based on user preferences. This feature sets Study Sync apart from other study apps by providing more than just study planning and helping students         find methods that suit their learning styles]

*   **Nice to Have (Post-Launch Backlog):**

    *   `Weekly and monthly planning`: [Allows students to organize their study schedules. It can wait as it is not essential for a study app to function although it is useful when given]

    *   `Study reminders`: [Sends reminders to help students stay consistent with their study schedules. It can wait as students can still manage their study sessions without automatic reminders]
